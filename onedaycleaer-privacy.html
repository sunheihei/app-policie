Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Privacy in brief. Sober Steps keeps your drinking records and journal on your device by default. You can start recording without creating a plan or an app account. Optional iCloud Sync stores selected records in your private CloudKit database. AI planning sends selected questionnaire answers through Cloudflare to DeepSeek only after your consent. Apple processes purchases, and RevenueCat processes purchase and limited technical information to manage Pro access and purchase analytics. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sober Steps, formerly One Day Clearer ("we", "us", or "the app"), handles information when you use our iOS app and associated services. The previous name may remain in App Store product identifiers and website addresses. The app is a behavior-support and record-keeping tool; it is not medical care.

1. Information the app stores

The app does not require an account. Your core app data is stored locally on your device using iOS app storage. This can include:

  • usual daily drink count, price per drink, selected currency, and, if you create a plan, your goal, drinking frequency, common trigger, and safety-check result;
  • daily drink counts, alcohol-free days, dates, optional check-in notes, plans, plant choice and growth, and reminder preferences;
  • journal text and moods, future letters, urge-support records, coping strategies, weekly reflections, saved encouragement, and journey milestones;
  • a trusted contact's name, phone number, and relationship, if you enter them manually; the app does not request access to your address book; and
  • app preferences, including whether you dismissed the home emergency-contact prompt, purchase-access status, AI-planning consent, and limited on-device feature-use timestamps included in an export you choose to make.

We do not operate an app account system. “Delete all local data” clears your saved profile, plan, check-ins, journal, related records, and manually entered contact on that device. Some device preferences, such as a dismissed home prompt and iCloud settings, can remain. Removing the app removes its local app storage; copies in device backups, iCloud, Apple Health, exports, or third-party services are separate. Deleting local records does not cancel a subscription or erase App Store or RevenueCat purchase records.

2. Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off by default. If you enable it, your profile, optional plan, plant choice, and daily check-ins, including any notes attached to those check-ins, sync through your private CloudKit database between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Journal moods are not included. This data is not shared with other Sober Steps users. The app stores its sync payload using CloudKit encrypted record values.

Journal entries, future letters, trusted contacts, urge-support records, weekly reviews, Health permissions, purchase status, AI consent, notification choices, and dismissed-prompt preferences are not part of this sync payload. Turning sync off stops future app sync but does not erase an existing iCloud copy. “Delete all local data” while sync is enabled also requests deletion of the synced payload; this requires an available iCloud account and a successful connection. Check the sync status in Settings. iCloud storage is governed by Apple's privacy practices and your Apple Account settings.

3. Optional widgets

The app writes a minimal on-device snapshot to an Apple App Group so an optional Sober Steps widget can display it. The snapshot can include whether today is recorded, today's plan target, a check-in streak, a plant stage, and a weekly recording and drink-count summary. It does not include journal text, future letters, notes, trusted-contact details, urge-support history, safety answers, or payment data. The widget does not send information to our servers.

4. Optional AI planning

Plans are optional and are created inside the app rather than during first-launch recording setup. AI is selected by default when you create a plan, but selected answers are sent only after you consent to AI data sharing. You can decline and use an on-device plan; an on-device alternative is also available if AI fails. Consent is saved locally. You can disable AI for the current plan in Settings; creating or regenerating an AI plan later may send a new request using your saved consent. “Delete all local data” clears that saved consent along with your records. The first seven plan days are available free and do not start a subscription; later plan days require Pro. These access rules do not change the data sent for a planning request.

What is sent when you agree

To create the requested plan, the app sends your goal, usual drinking frequency and drink count, common trigger, plan length, language, and safety-risk result. A legacy request may include equivalent drinking-baseline fields for compatibility. The planning request does not include journal text, daily check-ins, Apple Health data, payment information, price-per-drink estimates, currency, or trusted-contact details.

How that request is processed

Our Cloudflare Worker forwards the selected answers to DeepSeek to obtain a plan blueprint, then builds and validates the daily target schedule. The Worker keeps an IP-address-based request counter to prevent abuse; it expires automatically within approximately 61 minutes of creation. The Worker processes planning answers transiently rather than saving them as a user profile. Technical failure logs can include error codes, validation results, and response metadata. Providers may retain service or request information under the applicable service terms and privacy practices.

Cloudflare and DeepSeek process the transmitted information to provide AI planning. We require service providers that process information on our behalf to protect it in accordance with applicable law and at least the protections described in this policy. For information about their general privacy practices, see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and DeepSeek Privacy Policy. DeepSeek's public consumer policy does not cover downstream app users; our handling of your planning information is described here, and provider processing is subject to the applicable API service terms. Turning AI off does not remove a plan already saved or information previously processed by a provider.

5. Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. If you enable it and grant permission, Sober Steps writes the alcoholic beverage count from saved check-ins to Apple Health, replacing its own matching sample when updating a record. The app does not request permission to read or import Health data, and does not send Health data to AI providers or RevenueCat. You can disable future app writes in Settings and manage Health permission in the Health app or iOS Settings. Existing Health samples are separate from local app data.

6. Sharing, purchases, and service providers

Sharing you initiate

You can export local app-state data as JSON, share a journal PDF, or share a progress report as an image or PDF. An app-state export can contain sensitive records, including journal text, moods, notes, and planning information; preview it and choose recipients carefully. Progress-report images include summary totals, not private journal text or check-in notes. Manually entered trusted contacts are stored separately and are not included in the app-state JSON export. Sharing is initiated by you. Copies saved to Files or sent to another app, person, or service remain subject to that recipient's practices.

Purchases

Pro is available through a yearly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with the same features. Apple processes payments and provides verified transactions and access information. We do not receive your payment-card information. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing yearly subscription; manage renewal separately in your Apple Account subscription settings.

RevenueCat purchase management

The production app uses RevenueCat to validate and synchronize Pro access, restore purchases, and understand purchase activity. Its SDK can communicate with RevenueCat when the app launches, checks access, purchases, or restores, even if you have not yet purchased Pro. Information can include an automatically generated anonymous app-user identifier, transaction or receipt data, product identifiers, purchase dates, subscription expiration and access status, and limited technical information such as app version, device type, and operating system. Network requests also expose an IP address to the receiving service.

We do not send drinking records, journal text, moods, future letters, questionnaire answers, Apple Health data, or trusted-contact details to RevenueCat. We do not set your name, email address, or phone number as RevenueCat customer attributes. Purchase information is used for app functionality, restoration, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics, not cross-app advertising. RevenueCat's processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Purchase records may be retained as needed for these purposes and legal obligations; deleting local records does not remove them. Contact us for questions about service-side records. RevenueCat initialization and requests are disabled in Debug builds.

No advertising sale or behavioral tracking

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral advertising trackers in the app.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Local records: Use Settings → “Export a copy of my data” or “Delete all local data.” Some device preferences remain as described above.
  • iCloud Sync: Enable or disable it in Settings. Disable sync to stop future transfers; deletion of the remote payload requires deleting local data while sync is enabled and completing the iCloud request.
  • Journal entries: You can edit or delete individual entries in the app.
  • AI planning: Decline consent to use an on-device plan. Disable AI for an existing plan in Settings. This switch does not clear saved consent for later AI creation or regeneration; deleting all local data resets consent. Contact us about information already processed by the service.
  • Apple Health: Revoke access in Apple Health or iOS Settings. Removing the app does not automatically remove samples previously written to Apple Health.
  • Notifications: Manage notification permission in iOS Settings or turn reminders off in the app.
  • Trusted contact: Add, change, or remove it through the support screens. Closing the home prompt stores a local preference and does not delete a saved contact or contact anyone.
  • Purchases: Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account. Removing the app or deleting local data does not cancel renewal. Use Restore purchase to recover an eligible purchase.
  • Service-side information: Contact us about information processed by our services or providers. We handle applicable privacy requests and any required retention under applicable law; avoid sending sensitive diary or Health data with your request.

8. Security

We use iOS app-sandbox and App Group protections, CloudKit's private database and encrypted record values for optional sync, and HTTPS for service requests. Service providers may process information outside your country under their applicable terms and privacy safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and review your backup, iCloud, Health, and sharing settings.

9. Children and age requirement

Sober Steps is intended for adults. Do not use the app if you are under the legal drinking age where you live. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
